# Claims Denial Analytics Dashboard  
## Power BI Design Overview  
## Author: Raja Kalyanapu  

---

## 🎯 Purpose  
This dashboard provides a consolidated view of healthcare claim performance, denial patterns, and financial impact.  
It enables Revenue Cycle teams to identify top denial drivers and opportunities for process improvement or automation.

---

# 📊 Key Metrics (KPIs)

### **1. Total Claims**
- Count of all submitted claims

### **2. Denial Rate (%)**
- Percentage of denied claims  
Formula: `Denied Claims / Total Claims * 100`

### **3. Clean Claim Rate (%)**
- Claims accepted without edits

### **4. Financial Impact of Denials**
- Dollar value lost due to denied claims

### **5. Average Turnaround Time**
- Days from submission → adjudication

---

# 📈 Dashboard Visuals

### **1. Denial Trend Line Chart**
Shows denial rate over time — helps identify seasonal or operational patterns.

### **2. Top Denial Reasons (Bar Chart)**
- Eligibility  
- Coding  
- Documentation  
- Prior Authorization  
- Timely Filing  

Shows the percentage contribution of each reason.

### **3. Denials by Payer (Table / Bar Chart)**
Compares payer-specific denial behavior.

### **4. Denial Category Breakdown (Pie Chart)**
High-level distribution of denial categories.

### **5. Claim-Level Detail Table**
Includes:
- claim_id  
- payer  
- denial_reason  
- billed_amount  
- denial_amount  

---

# 🧠 Insights Provided  
- Root causes of frequent denials  
- High-risk payers  
- Trends in coding/documentation issues  
- Estimated revenue leakage  
- Impact of automation (RPA)
