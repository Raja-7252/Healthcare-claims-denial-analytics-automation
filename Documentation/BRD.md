# Business Requirements Document (BRD)
## Project: Healthcare Claims Denial Analytics & Automation  
## Author: Raja Kalyanapu  

---

## 1. Purpose  
Healthcare organizations face rising denial rates (10–25%), causing revenue loss and heavy manual rework.  
This project aims to analyze denial patterns, optimize workflows, and identify automation opportunities to reduce denials and improve clean claim rate.

---

## 2. Business Objectives  
- Reduce claim denial rate by **20–25%**  
- Improve clean claim rate by **15–20%**  
- Reduce manual rework steps by **30%**  
- Identify high-impact RPA opportunities  
- Provide leadership with actionable dashboards and insights  

---

## 3. Project Scope  

### **In Scope**
- Importing & cleaning claims dataset  
- Classifying denial reasons  
- Trend & KPI analysis  
- AS-IS workflow documentation  
- TO-BE optimized workflow  
- RPA feasibility assessment  
- Dashboard creation  
- Business recommendations  

### **Out of Scope**
- Integration with real EHR/EMR systems  
- HIPAA-protected data  
- Actual coding or claim adjudication  

---

## 4. Stakeholders  

| Stakeholder | Role | Responsibility |
|------------|------|----------------|
| Revenue Cycle Manager | Business Owner | Reviews denial trends & approves changes |
| Billing Specialist | SME | Provides process knowledge |
| BA / Data Analyst | You | Analysis, documentation, dashboards |
| Automation Team | RPA Engineers | Reviews feasibility & builds bots |
| IT Team | Data Support | Provides data extraction |

---

## 5. Business Requirements  
1. The system must analyze denial trends by type, payer, and timeframe.  
2. The dashboard must display clean claim rate, denial rate, top denial reasons, and financial impact.  
3. Workflow analysis must document AS-IS and propose TO-BE improvements.  
4. Automation opportunities must be evaluated with cost-benefit analysis.  
5. Reports must be exportable for stakeholder review.

---

## 6. Assumptions  
- Sample dataset is representative.  
- Claims process is similar to common healthcare RCM workflows.  
- KPI definitions align with industry standards.
