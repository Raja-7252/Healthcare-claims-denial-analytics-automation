# RPA Cost–Benefit Analysis  
## Healthcare Claims Denial Analytics & Automation  
## Author: Raja Kalyanapu  

---

## 💰 Assumptions  
- Bot development cost: **$8,000**  
- Bot maintenance cost: **$1,200/year**  
- Analyst hourly rate: **$28/hr**  
- Manual eligibility checks: **3 minutes per claim**  
- Claims per month: **14,000**  

---

# ⏳ Time Savings  

### **Before Automation**
14,000 claims × 3 min = **42,000 min/month**  
= **700 hours/month**

### **After Automation**
Bot handles **100%**  
Human handles exceptions (about 10%)  
≈ **70 hours/month**

### **Net Time Saved:**  
**630 hours/month**

---

# 💵 Cost Savings  

630 hours × $28/hr = **$17,640 saved per month**  
Annual = **$211,680 saved**

---

# 📈 ROI Calculation  

**Year 1 Savings:**  
$211,680 – (Development + Maintenance)  
$211,680 – $9,200 = **$202,480**

**ROI:**  
≈ **2,200%**

---

# ✔ Summary  
- Very high ROI  
- Manual work drastically reduced  
- RPA is strongly justified  
- Best starter automation for healthcare claims
